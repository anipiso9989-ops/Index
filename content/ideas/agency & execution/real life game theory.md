There are certain mechanisms determining success in this world.
1. Effort = linear, position = exponential. Leverage through people/capital/media/AI multiplies output.
2. Invisible competence is a hobby. Reality reacts on visible costly signals, not hidden talent.
3. Reality is indifferent to effort. It rewards perceived value and delivered outcomes, not hours. 
4. Track outcomes, not outputs; time/effort spent is a cost, not an accolade.
