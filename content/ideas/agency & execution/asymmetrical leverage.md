Progress is rarely linear; the biggest gains come from a few asymmetric, high-leverage actions.

Core Principles

- **Leverage > Volume**: One strategic move outperforms months of generic effort.
- **Find the Bend**: True progress requires identifying where inputs yield outsized outputs.
- **Contextual Scale**: A single technical adjustment in sports, strategy in business, or core principle in learning unlocks exponential results.
- **Motion vs. Progress**: Motion is linear and busy; leverage is exponential and decisive.

The ultimate goal is to stop being busy and find the fewest actions that move the entire system forward.
