An **agent** is an intelligence interacting with reality through a continuous loop:

- **Perceive & Understand**: Sense the environment.
- **Think**: Process & judge data.
- **Act**: Execute choices.

Feedback from ***action*** optimizes the ***thinking*** in the next loop (adaptation).
***
**Agency Maximization:** Agency is the ability to convert intention into reality. Formula: 

True Agency = Perception x Understanding x Judgement x Execution x Adaptation
