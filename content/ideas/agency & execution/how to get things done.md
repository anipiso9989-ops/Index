Work expands/compresses to fill the time allotted for its completion.

Given a week, it takes a week. Given a day, it takes a day. 

Lots of time → we procrastinate, and vice versa. Give yourself a deadline.

This process helps:
1. **Choose** the highest value action you know you can do properly and currently
2. **Clarify** what exactly you have to do; reduce it down to steps
3. **Commit** to it by using the above principle and actually start

And besides, if you know you have things to do eventually, why not just do them and relax later?
