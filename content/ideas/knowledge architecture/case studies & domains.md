# Domain Implementations: Math & History

## 1. Technical / Algorithmic Domains: The Cross Product Case
* **Surface Learning:** Memorizing coordinate calculation formulas:
  $$(u_2 v_3 - u_3 v_2, \; u_3 v_1 - u_1 v_3, \; u_1 v_2 - u_2 v_1)$$
  *(High decay risk, fragile under stress, zero generalization).*
* **Deep Understanding:** Modeling relational constituents: geometric orientation, perpendicularity in $\mathbb{R}^3$, area of the spanned parallelogram, and the right-hand rule.
* **Executable Concept Package:** Compressing this geometric intuition into an immediate operational mental primitive: a directional vector normal to the span, whose magnitude corresponds to planar area.
* **Application:** Executed in real time on vector calculus problems with minimal cognitive load and immediate transfer to physics (torque, angular momentum).

## 2. Complex / Non-Algorithmic Domains: The French Revolution Case
* **Agentic Failure Mode:** An initial rule of "preserve only actionable primitives" discards historical nuance in favor of shallow heuristics (e.g., "how to prevent revolt"). The underlying reality cannot be recovered from the flattened rule.
* **Reconstructive Resolution:** Preserve causal inflection points, structural/institutional debt constraints, key factional dynamics, and chronological anchors.
* **Outcome:** The causal model faithfully mirrors reality, allowing transferable political and systemic insights to be derived on demand without compromising the factual integrity of the history.
