atomic knowledge is essentially the smallest, most simplest unit of knowledge, that is still interpretable. 

1 atomic knowledge unit is 1 **standalone, self-contained** concept. But this concept MUST be unique, and standalone. it can have interactions and relations with other units, but the key thing is that in order for it to be an atomic knowledge unit, it must offer knowledge that no other unit offers.

I believe that knowledge should be organized and presented in its most simplest form, so that it can be synthesized, combined, and used to achieve things.

Combine this with [[agentic knowledge]], and you can curate a knowledge collection that's really valuable.

Atomic knowledge units act as atoms that can be combined into larger molecules. They are structures that define a set of concepts, esssentially.

In order to determine the relationship between atoms in a molecule, you can use the following operators, and visualize atoms and their molecules like you do in chemistry.
"-" = conjunction 
"×" = interaction 
"→" = causation

A molecule has achieved maximum simplicity if removing any one atom would cause the molecule to be incomplete.
