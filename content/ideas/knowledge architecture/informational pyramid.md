Value is not distributed evenly across the spectrum of information. It scales exponentially as you climb the Knowledge Pyramid (from bottom to top):

- **Data:** Raw, unorganized facts (noise).
- **Information:** Structured, cleaned data.
- **Knowledge:** Integrated information put into context.
- **Applied Knowledge:** Knowledge put into action.
- **Results:** The real-world feedback and value generated by that action.

Most people spend their entire lives drowning at the bottom of the stack, consuming endless streams of data and information. But the market does not reward consumption; it rewards execution. Value is created almost entirely at the top of the stack. Stop collecting data. Start applying knowledge to produce results.
