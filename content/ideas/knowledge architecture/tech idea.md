The eventual ideal device for note taking (as outlined in my [[random note taking hypothesis]]):

- Pocket-sized.
- E-ink.
- Stylus + rear eraser.
- Low-latency handwriting.
- Optional handwriting → text.
- PDF / EPUB / TXT / Markdown.
- Import/export.
- Search/OCR.
- Sync.
- Frontlight/night light.
- Long battery life.

Philosophy:

> **Paper's cognitive experience + digital information's portability.**

Not a miniature tablet.

**Think. Read. Write.**
