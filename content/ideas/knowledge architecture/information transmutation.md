Information becomes knowledge when understood.

Knowledge becomes wisdom when applied.

Wisdom becomes intuition when repeatedly applied across time.

Most people consume information endlessly but never allow it to crystallize into higher forms.

Information (raw facts) -> Knowledge (organized understanding) -> Wisdom (direct application) -> Intuition (automatic recognition)

Progress requires moving through this chain of events, not merely accumulating information.

The objective of learning is not information acquisition, but its crystallization. True mastery occurs when correct action becomes automatic.
