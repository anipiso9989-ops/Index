Fiction books are inherently meant to be enjoyed; they *should* be long, prose, and descriptive. Fiction pulls the reader into a new world, and to do so the book needs to reflect that need.

Nonfiction, on the other hand, is mostly just actionable advice or information. We read nonfiction to get something out of it, to learn something. These should *not* be long. Nonfiction should be as compressed and as short as possible, so that the reader can quickly learn the information, and apply it. This applies to any nonfiction book or piece of literature.
