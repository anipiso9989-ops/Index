***
**Agentic knowledge** is knowledge that enables action, that no other knowledge can. 

By focusing on extracting only agentic knowledge, your knowledge base becomes more efficient, concise, and actionable. No wasted space, or clutter.

In the real world, informational knowledge is of no use. The value of knowledge is measured by its actionability and its adaptability.

Keep only that which is truly valuable, and regularly update it to maintain its reliability.

Not everything consumed needs to be agentic. Key distinction here: *consumed*, not *kept*. Sometimes, just being aware about certain things can enrich your life.

The value of a piece of agentic knowledge is determined by its yield.

$$
\text{Yield} = \frac{\text{Problems Solved}}{\text{Frequency of Real World Use}}
$$

Combine this with [[atomic knowledge]], and you can curate a knowledge collection that's really valuable.
