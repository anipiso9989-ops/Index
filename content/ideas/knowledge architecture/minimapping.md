
***
**Minimapping** is nonlinear note-taking focused on extracting only *agentic knowledge*—information that enables action—from a single high-quality source, and representing it in the most minimal, lossless form.

**Process:**
1. **Choose one strong source**  
    Find a comprehensive, high-quality resource. Don’t chase perfection or over-search.
2. **Filter for [[agentic knowledge]]**  
    Only keep what directly helps you do something (rules, methods, key insights). Ignore excess explanation and jargon.
3. **Build a nonlinear map**  
    Represent concepts as nodes and connect them. Keep everything compressed but fully understandable.

**Core idea:**  
Minimapping is lossless knowledge compression—removing everything non-essential while preserving what enables execution.
