# Core Architecture & Pipeline

## Definition
**Concept packaging** is the cognitive compilation process through which raw learning material is understood in real time, modeled by its relational constituent structure, and compressed into a compact, executable mental representation that preserves information across time.

Primary Axiom: **Preservation first, efficiency second.**

---

## The Compilation Pipeline

Learning functions analogously to compiling software:
1. Learning Material
2. Deep Understanding (Relational constituent modeling)
3. Concept Packaging (Compression via preservation constraints)
4. Executable Knowledge (Bytecode / cached runtime primitives)
5. Application (Fast, low-latency execution & generalization)
### Stage Breakdown
1. **Raw Material:** External inputs, proofs, data, or narrative sequences.
2. **Deep Understanding:** Constructing a causal model of the subject's constituent parts and their mutual relations rather than indexing surface procedures or disconnected facts.
3. **Concept Packaging:** Compressing that structural understanding into an optimal minimal set of nodes while enforcing derivability.
4. **Executable Knowledge:** The compiled representation ready to be "run" on practice problems, real-world tasks, or novel edge cases.
5. **Application:** Low-overhead execution with high generalization power.

---

## Optimization Hierarchy

A valid concept package optimizes along this strict priority order:

1. **Information Preservation**
   All critical information must remain present or derivable.
   $$I_{\text{important}} \subseteq I_{\text{stored}} \cup I_{\text{reconstructable}}$$

2. **Reconstructability**
   Derivability is defined by the learner's actual ability to recover the idea in practice, not theoretical mathematical derivation.

3. **Temporal Robustness**
   The representation must withstand realistic cognitive decay and retain derivability after extended periods of non-use.

4. **Compression**
   Once preservation and recovery are locked, strip unnecessary representations to reduce cognitive storage load.

5. **Computational Efficiency (Execution Latency)**
   Among candidate packages that preserve equal information, prefer the structure requiring minimal inference time and cognitive strain during runtime execution.

6. **Strategic Redundancy**
   Retain non-minimal components when they protect against retrieval failure, reduce execution latency, or offer alternative retrieval pathways.
