# The Cognitive Loop and Action

Capability, goals, and world models are passive components. For intelligence to manifest, the system must execute actions. 

### Reasoning as Action
Reasoning can be conceptualized as an internal cognitive action. However, whether "reasoning" is broad enough to encompass all intelligent cognitive operations remains an open question.

### The General Cognitive Loop
The interaction between an intelligent agent and its environment can be modeled as a continuous feedback loop:

$$ \text{Goal} \rightarrow \text{Model} \rightarrow \text{Prediction} \rightarrow \text{Action} \rightarrow \text{Feedback} \rightarrow \text{Model update} $$

- **Goal:** Defines the objective and relevance criteria.
- **Model:** Represents the current state of the world.
- **Prediction:** Simulates potential consequences of actions.
- **Action:** Executes the selected behavior (internal or external).
- **Feedback:** Observes the environmental response.
- **Model Update:** Adapts the internal representation based on the discrepancy between prediction and feedback.
