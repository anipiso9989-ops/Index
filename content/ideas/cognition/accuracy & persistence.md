### Accuracy
A person can learn an inaccurate or false model. Therefore:

**Learning ≠ correctness.**

Accuracy is a property of the learned model and determines whether resulting outputs correspond to reality. Updating a model in response to evidence can preserve or improve its accuracy.

### Persistence
A transient state change does not necessarily constitute learning. Learning requires enough persistence for a stable capacity to exist.

Persistence is not necessarily permanent. A learned capability can later be forgotten. The exact structural threshold for “persistent enough” remains open.
