***
**Creative thinking** is generating new possibilities.

Break patterns, figure out alternatives.

Explore freely in your mind.
***
Combine this with [[minimalism]], and you get **minimal creative thinking**. The goal is to find the simplest and most elegant solution to the problem.

I've built a general template:
1. Define the objective or constraint
2. Generate many possible approaches
3. Combine and transform promising ideas as needed
4. Select and refine the strongest one
