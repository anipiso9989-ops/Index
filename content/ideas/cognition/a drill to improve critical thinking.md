***
Takes 10 mins daily for substantial results.

1. **Claim**: State one sentence with % confidence (factual, personal, or policy).
2. **Assumptions**: List 2–3 required truths.
3. **Counterargument**: Find the strongest opposing factual evidence.
4. **Update Rule**: Define what specific evidence changes your mind.
5. **First Principles**: Reduce to core fundamental mechanisms.
6. **Re-evaluate**: State your new % confidence and why.

Rules
- **Objective**: Optimize for truth, not validation.
- **Calibration**: Choose harder claims if confidence rarely changes.
- **Tracking**: Record every drill.
