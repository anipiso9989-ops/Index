Metathinking means observing, analyzing, and regulating your own cognitive processes to upgrade and simplify them.

You must keep your mind clean and regulated, treating it like a personal living space.

Achieve awareness by writing down your current thoughts on paper to prevent brain overload.

Assess thought utility to determine if thinking is helping achieve goals or acting as [[passive vs active work]].

Adjust thought processes consciously to simplify and modify your life.

This serves as a practical application of [[adaptive thinking]] because you must know the software before you can modify it.

This process also optimizes your mind via [[cognitive load & clearing]], pruning useless thoughts to free you from wasting mental energy.
