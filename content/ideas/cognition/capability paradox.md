Historically, human achievement was bottle-necked by **technical capability** and execution barriers.

Now, AI and automation will soon allow for practically infinite capability, dissolving the execution bottleneck. The core constraint shifts from **how to build** to **what to build**.

When execution is free, the ultimate skills become **taste, curation, and judgment**. The definitive question changes from how to achieve a goal to: **"What is worth wanting?"**
