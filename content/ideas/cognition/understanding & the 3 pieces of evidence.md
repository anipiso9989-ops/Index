## Understanding
Current provisional definition:

> **Understanding a model = knowing how its concepts and relationships interact.**

Learning concerns acquiring/modifying the actual model; understanding concerns the structure and interactions within that model.

This distinction remains an open theoretical question.

## The 3 Pieces of Evidence
These are the 3 major pieces of evidence I believe that someone who has learned something should be able to prove their learning; not necessarily all of them, but at least one.

### Use
Use is applying an existing model to perform a task, including unfamiliar instances. Novelty of the problem does not automatically mean the model itself was extended.

### Teaching
Teaching is evidence that a learner has sufficiently organized knowledge to communicate it, but teaching ability is not the only possible evidence of learning and can be limited by communication skill.

### Extension
Extension means discovering or generating new concepts or relationships from an existing model.

A genuinely new theorem derived from existing knowledge is an example of extension.
