## Model Construction vs. Model Optimization
Learning can involve both:

- **Construction:** adding or altering informational/functional content.
- **Optimization:** making an existing model faster, more efficient, reliable, flexible, or effective.

Optimization can add derived internal information without requiring new external information. Example: recognizing A→D directly when A→B→C→D was already known can be viewed as compression/derived structure.
