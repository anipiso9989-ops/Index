***
**Systems thinking** is understanding interactions.

Look at the whole, focus on how parts affect each other.

Think in causes/effects, and interactions.
***
Applying [[minimalism]], we get **minimal systems thinking**. The goal is to design the simplest system to accomplish a task.

I've built a general template:
1. Define the goal
2. Find the minimum set of structured actions that reliably accomplishes it
3. Measure the outcomes that matter
4. Remove everything that doesn't improve those outcomes
