***
**Critical thinking** is rigorous evaluation.

Analyze everything, focus on what's true.

Remove bias, emotion, etc...
***
Combine this with [[minimalism]], and you get **minimal critical thinking**. The goal is to build the simplest evaluation.

I've built a general template:
1. Identify the claim
2. Evaluate all available evidence and reasoning that supports the claim
3. Look for any weaknesses, alternatives, biases
4. Reach the best supported conclusion with the least amount of evidence/reasoning
