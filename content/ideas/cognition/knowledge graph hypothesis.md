## Knowledge Graph Hypothesis
A person's learned knowledge can be provisionally represented as one large informational graph.

Learning may alter its structure by:
- adding concepts/nodes;
- adding relationships/edges;
- strengthening or weakening associations;
- reorganizing structure;
- connecting previously separate regions;
- compressing information;
- optimizing pathways for use;
- updating or replacing inaccurate structures.

This is currently a **theoretical abstraction**, not an established claim about literal brain architecture.
