Reasoning can be modeled as a system of four core components:

- **Input**: A representation of concepts and their relations.
- **Transformation**: Operations that change one mental representation into another.
- **Constraints**: Rules, assumptions, objectives, or consistency requirements that limit valid transformations.
- **Output**: A new cognitive state (e.g., solution, explanation, prediction, proof, question, or progress).

This architecture mirrors the feedback loop found in [[agency process & maximization]] and represents the mechanics of [[simple systems]].
