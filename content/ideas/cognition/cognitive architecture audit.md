Periodic mental refactoring eliminates outdated beliefs and conflicting systems that cause friction.

- Identify systems, beliefs, or ideas that act as cognitive opposites.
- Determine if conflicts stem from low skill or subconscious contradictions.
- Reconcile the contradiction or delete the obsolete model.
- Delete any model you would not keep if building your mind from scratch.
- Consciously prune your dynamic mental architecture to prevent ideas from becoming noise.
- Maintain constant version control because [[simple systems]] require active maintenance.
- Apply [[metathinking]] as the absolute prerequisite for this process.
