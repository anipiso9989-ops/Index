- True certainty backed by irrefutable evidence is wisdom and accurate alignment with reality
- The enemy of intelligence is false, unjustified certainty, not confidence itself
- Humility and doubt are not identical; the mind should stay open when evidence is incomplete but close the case when reality becomes clear
- Curiosity, not dogma, is the correct posture when information remains incomplete
- Proportional certainty must scale directly with available evidence to protect against both gullibility and paralysis
- The ultimate definition of wisdom is knowing when evidence is sufficient and realizing that continued doubt is no longer intellectually honest
