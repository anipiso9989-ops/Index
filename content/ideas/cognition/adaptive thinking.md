***
**Adaptive thinking** is updating according to reality.

Detach from everything, focus on adapting.

Adjust quickly without slowing down or losing direction.
***
Combine this with [[minimalism]], and you get **minimal adaptive thinking**. The goal is to adapt the minimal amount to keep making progress.

I've built a general template:
1. Observe what has changed
2. Update your understanding as sparingly as possible
3. Adjust your actions as sparingly as possible
4. Repeat continuously
