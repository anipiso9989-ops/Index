***
**Fundamental thinking** (also called *first principles thinking*) is reducing things to core truths. 

Ignore everything, focus on the truth and the fundamentals.

Simplify as much as possible.
***
Combining this with [[minimalism]], we get **minimal fundamental thinking**. The goal is to build the simplest solution (even though fundamental thinking itself is inherently simple, we can go one step further).

I've built a general template:
1. Define the problem
2. Focus on facts, not assumptions/lies
3. Reduce the problem to the least amount of fundamental truths
4. Rebuild the solution from those truths
