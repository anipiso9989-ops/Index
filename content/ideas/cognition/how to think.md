***
Thinking is the iterative construction, evaluation, and refinement of internal models that improve an agent's ability to predict and influence reality.

- **Perceive** — Gather accurate information (signal over noise).
- **Model** — Compress reality into principles and mental models.
- **Reason** — Predict consequences and derive implications.
- **Decide** — Choose the highest-leverage action under constraints.
- **Reflect** — Compare prediction with reality; update the model.

Of course, you don't want to overthink. If you do, check out [[how to stop overthinking]].

Thinking, though, is only the middle layer in the [[agency process & maximization]].
