
***
**Meditation** is controlled awareness, not forced silence.

Focus on an anchor (e.g., breath). Let thoughts arise; ignore them and return. The mind calms itself.

This is training your attention -> you'll gain the ability to choose your response & reprogram your mind over time.

Meditation helps to bolster your [[cognitive sovereignty]], and it also is a way to receive intuition (see [[manifestation]]).

I think that meditation is a form of internal hygiene; ≥ 15-20 minutes daily to clean the nervous system.
