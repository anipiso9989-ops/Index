To produce valuable work, you must work deeply and live simply. Divide your day into strict focus and relaxation periods (with buffer time in the work period) to maximize quality over quantity.

- **Deep Work**: Allocate undistracted, single-tasking time to execute what really matters, refusing the illusion of superficial busyness (see [[passive vs active work]]).
- **Slow Living**: Reduce your daily physical and mental load during rest periods to preserve long-term cognitive quality.
- **The Blueprint**: Precision in focus, patience in output.
