Modern environments overwhelm our finite internal attention with infinite external information. The mind must function as a clear, focused consciousness rather than a cluttered storage workspace.
1. Outsource storage to external systems to protect your nervous system. You do the computations.
2. Compress and express thoughts externally to test and sharpen your true understanding.
3. Think first, then externalize the result. Tools should assist your analysis, never replace your thinking.

Mind should direct reality, not store it.

High cognitive load clutters your mind and life. Clarity is paramount; protect it by externalizing everything immediately. Record ideas, stories, tasks, procedures, or thoughts in the real world (paper or apps) to empty your brain.
