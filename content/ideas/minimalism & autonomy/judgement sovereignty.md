You can outsource almost anything in the modern world. You can hire consultants to analyze your business, trainers to design your workouts, and mentors to give you advice. But there is one thing you can never outsource: responsibility.

No external system, no matter how advanced or intelligent, has access to your unique internal state—your risk tolerance, your deepest values, your fears, and your personal context. Advice is cheap because the person giving it doesn't have to live with the consequences of execution. Listen to mentors, gather data, and consult the experts. But when the trigger must be pulled, the decision is yours alone. Own your judgment. You are the sovereign of your own life.
