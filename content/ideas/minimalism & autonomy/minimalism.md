
***
Minimalism $\neq$ emptiness; = precision.

It does not meaning removing character/quality. What remains must be exceptional. Keep only that which is high-caliber. Minimalism is valuable because it allows you to focus. All great people throughout history lived a simple life, to direct their energy towards mastery.

Apply minimalism everywhere: physically, digitally, mentally. 
- **Physically** remove everything without value; keep only what's meaningful/useful.
- **Digitally** stay away from social media, cluttered workspaces, excess info.
- **Mentally** stop feeding useless thoughts; stay present and ignore them.

Minimalism is not emptiness. It is precision.

Put into practice, we get [[simple systems]].
