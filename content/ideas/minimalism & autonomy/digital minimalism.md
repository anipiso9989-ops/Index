Digital minimalism and offline habits protect your focus from digital distractions. We live in an attention economy, so everything on the internet is gonna fight for your focus. Don't give in.

- Purge Devices: Strip apps down to the bare minimum. Organize everything.
- Treat Web Use as a Mission: Enter the internet with an objective, get out immediately.
- Work Locally: Store everything you can locally to remove internet dependence.
- Activate night mode to block blue light.
