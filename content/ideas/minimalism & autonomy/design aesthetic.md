# Design Style: Technical Intimacy

The intersection of system-level thinking and personal narrative. Warm, analog, human.

---

Color Palette

|Role|Hex|Feel|
|---|---|---|
|Background (light)|`#FAEBD6`|Warm cream/parchment|
|Background (dark)|`#858D85`|Muted sage|
|Deep accent|`#33221B`|Espresso brown|
|Left accent bar|`#7D7671`|Warm taupe|
|Soft highlight|`#FFD6C0`|Warm peach (occasional)|

Avoid: pure whites, bright primaries, cool blues.

---

Typography

Monospace (Anonymous Pro or similar). Signals raw, direct, unfiltered. Bold for headlines (uppercase), regular for body, italic for emphasis. Contrast with serif/sans only for deliberate moments.

---

Imagery

Natural textures: aged paper, concrete, weathered wood, stone, foggy forests. Atmospheric: moody, desaturated, soft focus, dawn light, light through leaves. Real: candid, behind-the-scenes, evidence of process. Abstract: marbled paper, watercolor washes, grain, subtle imperfections.

Avoid: polished stock, corporate imagery, pure blacks/brights, geometric flat art.

---

Layout

Hero + Canvas (dramatic dark opening → warm light content). Asymmetry (left accent bars, directional gradients, uneven margins). Generous negative space. Horizontal light shift (darker left, brighter right).
