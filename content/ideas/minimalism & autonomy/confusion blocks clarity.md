Nobody is truly as confused as they think; they are comfortable with pretending to be ignorant, using confusion as a cover to avoid responsibility.

Most people do actually know the next step, but they refuse to take it.

Clarity comes with a cost. It *reveals* the price of commitment, risk, and action. Stop protecting the pattern, and *act*.

Overthinking exacerbates this, leading to a loop of thinking as a substitute for action. Your understanding of the situation may improve, but no action has been taken.

Act on the thing you cannot deny. It destroys fantasy and shows you the reality. Only then can you act with clarity, and position yourself on the path to achieve that which you wish.
