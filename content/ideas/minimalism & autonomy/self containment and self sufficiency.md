***
- **Self-containment** = internal organization, coherence, completeness.
- **Self-sufficiency** = external independence from fragile systems.
***
I admire self-contained things: ideas that explain themselves, rooms without clutter, people who do not leak chaos into every environment. Self-containment creates precision. Nothing extra. Nothing dependent on constant correction. It is quiet strength—the ability to remain whole without demanding attention, validation, or external scaffolding.

Dependence creates fragility. Self-sufficiency creates leverage. The more needs outsourced to unstable systems, the less freedom remains under pressure. I value learning to build, repair, think, and adapt independently—not to reject others, but to avoid helplessness. Competence is freedom. Resilience begins where dependency ends.

However, complete independence may create vulnerability. It is wise to maintain some connection to the external world to see if you can get something out of it; the idea is to maintain independence but still interact with the world.
