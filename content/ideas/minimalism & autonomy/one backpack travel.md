***
Keeping everything self contained (see [[self containment and self sufficiency]]), so that you can focus on enjoying your trip rather than worrying about your luggage and how much you have. Minimalist traveling is important for this very reason. It take a major burden off your shoulders, regarding how to manage all of your possessions.
***
This sample packing list is optimized for the *Pakt One Travel Bag, 35 L*. Allows one to travel **anywhere, indefinitely**.

### Worn on Transit (Bulkiest Items)
- **Boots:** GORUCK MACV-1 or Danner Tachyon (rugged, lightweight, fast-drying).
- **Pants:** Western Rise Evolution or Outlier Slim Dungarees (stain-proof nylon).
- **Shirt:** 100% Merino Wool T-Shirt (Proof or Outlier).
- **Layer:** Patagonia R1 Air Crew or merino hoodie.
- **Jacket:** Arc'teryx Beta LT or Patagonia Torrentshell shell.

### Left Side Compartment (Capsule Wardrobe)
- **T-Shirts (2):** Wool & Prince or Proof 72-Hour Merino (Black, Grey).
- **Long Sleeve (1):** Merino wool button-down or henley (Duckworth/Icebreaker).
- **Shorts/Swim (1):** Myles Apparel Everyday or Outlier New Way shorts.
- **Underwear (3):** ExOfficio Give-N-Go 2.0 or Arc'teryx Motus boxers.
- **Socks (3):** Darn Tough Light Hiker Quarter Crew (merino).
- **Insulation (1):** Patagonia Micro Puff or Arc'teryx Atom LT (packable puffy).
- **Shoes (1):** Vivobarefoot Primus Lite or Xero Shoes (flat-packing minimalists).

### Right Side Compartment (Tech & Hardware)
- **Laptop:** 14" or 16" MacBook Air/Pro (fits Pakt's center sleeve).
- **Power Bank:** Anker 737 or Prime (high-capacity laptop charger).
- **Adapter:** Minix Neo P1 or 65W GaN multi-port USB-C wall charger.
- **Cables (2):** Heavy-duty braided USB-C to USB-C cords.
- **Headphones:** Apple AirPods Pro.
- **Documents:** Passport & phone (stored in Pakt’s quick-access pocket).

### Utilities & Everyday Carry
- **Headlamp:** Petzl Bindi or Nitecore NU25 (USB-C rechargeable).
- **Water Bottle:** Memobottle A6 (flat profile to prevent side-pocket bulge).
- **Sunglasses:** Huckberry Weekenders or Oakley Frogskins.
- **Towel:** Matador Ultralight Travel Towel (nanofiber).
- **Laundry:** Sea to Summit Pocket Laundry Leaves + universal sink stopper.

### Toiletry Kit (Mesh Dividers / TSA Pouch)
- **Case:** Matador FlatPak Toiletry Case (breathable fabric dries items while closed).
- **Hygiene:** Solid cologne, Matador soap bars, solid toothpaste tabs, travel deodorant.
- **Grooming:** Stainless steel nail clippers and tweezers.
- **First Aid:** Micro-ziplock with strip bandages, ibuprofen, blister pads, Imodium.

> **Core Strategy:** You travel indefinitely with 4 pairs of socks, 3 boxers, 2 shirts, and 1 pair of pants total (including worn items). Wash in a sink every 3–4 days. Start trip with Pakt's 5L expansion zipper closed to save space.
