Sustainability should be a feature of society, not an add-on.

Debates are going on whether climate change is real, or just a hoax for governments/companies to make money.

Whether it's real or not, the protection of the natural world (in all aspects), and the reversal of the damage that humans have done to it, is important.

When humanity launches off into the stars, we should leave the Earth much better than we found it.
