The Entropy-Efficiency Threshold is the invisible line in any system where adding more features or organization actually starts to hurt you. Every system creates "entropy"—the energy you have to spend just to keep the system running. When the work required to maintain your system is greater than the value the system provides, you have crossed the threshold. Instead of trying to optimize further, you must use [[complexity relocation]] to simplify or [[concisification]] to strip the system back to its core.

This is the theory behind [[maintenance as the hidden price of ownership]], [[results per unit of complexity]], and [[efficiency of complexity]].
