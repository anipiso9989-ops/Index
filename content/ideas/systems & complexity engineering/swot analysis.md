Generalize SWOT by stripping away its original business framing:

**SWOT = a 2×2 analysis of helpful/harmful × internal/external factors.**

|              | Helpful       | Harmful    |
| ------------ | ------------- | ---------- |
| **Internal** | Strengths     | Weaknesses |
| **External** | Opportunities | Threats    |

So for **anything**—a person, idea, project, decision, system, argument, strategy—ask:

* **Strengths:** What inherent properties help it succeed?
* **Weaknesses:** What inherent properties limit it?
* **Opportunities:** What external conditions could be exploited?
* **Threats:** What external conditions could undermine it?

The deeper abstraction is simply: **evaluate an object by its internal capabilities/limitations and its external advantages/risks.**
