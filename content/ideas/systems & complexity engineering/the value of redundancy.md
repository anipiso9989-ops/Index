# Generalized Redundancy

## Definition
Intentionally building backup into systems so failures do not cascade.

## Examples
- Spare batteries.
- Multiple file backups.
- Two routes to work.
- Emergency fund.
- Multiple marketable skills.

## Benefits
- Increases resilience.
- Prevents single points of failure.
- Reduces stress during disruptions.
- Buys time for deliberate responses.

## Tradeoff
Too little redundancy creates fragility.
Too much redundancy wastes resources.

## Principle
A small amount of slack compounds into long-term robustness.
