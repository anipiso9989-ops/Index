This is a cool idea I found. 

Basically, the goal is to shadow your target language's communication, in both writing and speaking. 

For speaking, you listen to continuous **target language** audio through earphones and simultaneously speak the target language aloud at the exact same speed, rhythm, and intonation.

For writing, you hand-write the **target language** text line-by-line while speaking it aloud, establishing visual and physical memory of its script and syntax.

You can refer to translations in your native languages as needed, but the goal is to phase out the reliance on it gradually.
