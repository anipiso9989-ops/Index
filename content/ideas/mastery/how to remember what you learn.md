To remember what you learn, combine the techniques of spaced repetition & active recall into what I call **Spaced Active Recall**

> **Spaced Active Recall**: Repeatedly reconstructing knowledge from memory at optimally increasing intervals.

Active recall determines how you study for retention, spaced repetition determines when. Together, they form **one** tool that works to give you the best results.

Use flashcard apps to practically apply this principle.
