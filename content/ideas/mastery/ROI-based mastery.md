Every skill you decide to learn requires an investment of your most scarce resource: time. You must treat skill acquisition like a cold, calculating investor.

The value of any skill can be calculated using a simple formula:

$$\text{Skill Value} = \frac{\text{Lifetime Benefit}}{\text{Aquisition Cost}}$$

Do not shy away from highly difficult skills. Often, the skills with the highest acquisition cost (like mathematics, deep technical programming, or high-stakes negotiation) have lifetime benefits that scale exponentially, completely dwarfing the initial cost. Conversely, avoid easy-to-learn skills that offer low leverage and rapid obsolescence. Run the math before you commit your attention.
