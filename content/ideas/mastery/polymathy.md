I think being a polymath, that is, excelling in numerous distinct disciplines, is valuable. 

It makes you a more well-rounded person.

For example, being good at math/physics, chess, and violin. This is a simple, yet powerful combination. 

In order to become a polymath, conserve and direct your energy towards your interests. Ignore EVERYTHING that doesn't serve them.

Put into practice, you get [[the theory of generalized learning development]].
