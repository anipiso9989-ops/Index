**Autodidactism** is self-directed learning independent of formal schooling. It builds true mastery at your own pace. 

The word's origin comes from Greek.
"auto", meaning self. "didactic" meaning to teach.

This is valuable because you can learn at your own speed, and you can experiment how you want. It also builds independence through self reliance.

To teach yourself something, follow the process below, but adapt it to whatever you're learning:
1. Isolate: agentic knowledge only
2. Model: study elite examples
3. Act: deliberate practice
4. Test: real feedback
5. Refine: adapt + repeat

Combining this with some of my other ideas like [[the theory of generalized learning development]], [[intentional learning]], and [[progressive intellectual overload]] means that you can essentially develop much faster than most people.
