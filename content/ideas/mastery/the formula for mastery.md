
***
$$
\text{M} = \text{R} \times\text{S} \times \text{P}
$$
$\text{M}$ is mastery. Doing anything consistently at a high level.

$\text{R}$ is recovery. Resting, reflecting, adapting.

$\text{S}$ is sharpening. Deliberate practice, analysis, feedback, application (make a project testing everything you've learned).

$\text{P}$ is purpose. Your single "why".
