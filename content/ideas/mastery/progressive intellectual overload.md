The principle of progressive overload, famously used in bodybuilding, states that you must continually increase a task's difficulty to make progress.

This is illustrated by the ancient legend of Milo of Croton. By carrying a newborn calf every day, his strength grew seamlessly as the animal matured into a heavy bull. By pushing just past your current limits, you extend them.

This philosophy applies directly to learning. To truly master a skill, you must actively apply your knowledge rather than just consuming information. Once you complete a challenge, immediately move to a harder one.

For example, when learning the piano, do not just study sheet music. Start by playing an easy piece, then build your skills through experience by steadily increasing the difficulty and variety of the music.
