# Welcome!

This is the homepage for Index, a place where I can show and grow my ideas. This is the culmination of ~3-4 years of long thinking during road trips and also bike rides.

The best way to see all my ideas is to press "Ctrl + G" on your keyboard, or press the tiny icon on the top right corner of the box (looks like a graph). It'll show you all my ideas as a graph.

Click any circle to open an idea, and zoom in/out as needed.

Disclaimer: This is a personal collection of ideas/models/hypotheses/observations. Most of them are in progress and should not be interpreted as fact. They represent how I currently understand/investigate the subjects I'm working on. Where appropriate, I distinguish between established knowledge, personal models, and open questions.
